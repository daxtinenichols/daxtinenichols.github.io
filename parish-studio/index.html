Put it online, free

Your site is a single file. Any free web host can show it to the world. This is the simplest way we know.

  1. Download your site

    Press Download my site. You'll get a file named index.html. Press Save my details too, and keep that file somewhere safe, so you or the next volunteer can update the site later.

  2. Upload it to a free host

    Use one of the two hosts below. Both are free for a small site like this, and both give you a web address right away.

  3. Share the address

    Put it in the bulletin, on the parish sign, and on your Google Maps listing, so people searching "Mass near me" find your times. Once it's up, the free Parish Scheduler can keep your lectors, servers and Eucharistic ministers organized.

Netlify Drop the easiest

  1. Put index.html into its own folder on your computer.
  2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag that folder onto the page.
  3. Make a free account when it asks, so your site stays online. You can rename the address in its settings.

To update: drag the new folder onto your site's Deploys page.

GitHub Pages

  1. Make a free account at github.com.
  2. Create a new public repository named yourname.github.io (using your account name).
  3. Choose uploading an existing file, add index.html, and press Commit changes.

Your site appears at that same address within a few minutes. To update, upload the new file the same way. GitHub's own guide

Good to know

Does anyone else see what I type?

No. Everything stays in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else until you upload the finished file yourself.

Our parish has two or three churches. Does that work?

Yes. In step 3, press Add another church for each one. Every church gets its own address, map, photo and Mass and confession times. Visitors can read the times church by church, or all together by day so they can find the next Mass anywhere in the parish.

Can I change the look later?

Any time. Pick another template in step 1 and everything you typed carries over. You can also change the color, the heading style and the text size, and in step 7 turn sections on or off or move them around.

How do I change the Mass times later?

Come back to this page on the same device and your details will still be here. On a different device, press Open saved details and choose the details file you saved. Details saved with the earlier version of the Studio open too. Change what you need, download again, and upload the new file the way you did the first time.

Can we use our own web address, like stbrigid.org?

Yes. Both Netlify and GitHub Pages let you connect a domain you own, free of charge. Buying the domain itself usually costs around $10 to $20 a year from any registrar.

Do we need permission from the diocese?

Policies differ. Many dioceses have guidance for parish websites, so it's worth checking with your pastor and your diocesan communications office before you share the address.

Why are the newcomer guides suggested?

People often look up a parish right before their first visit. A short, kind guide to the Mass and a place to explore honest questions can make that first step easier. They're free, ad-free, and you can turn any of them off in step 8.

What about photos of people?

Only post photos of people who have agreed to it, and never post photos of children without their parents' permission. Large photos are shrunk automatically so the site stays quick.
