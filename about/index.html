The Tree of Life project

About, privacy and credits

Five free tools that help people find and grow in the Catholic faith. Here is who makes them, how they were built, what they keep about you, and whose work they rely on.

Who makes this Privacy Not an official source Credits Contact

One person, with Claude

This is a one-person project. Every tool here is planned, chosen and published by one Catholic, in spare time, with no organization, company or money behind it.

Most of the work is done with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Claude wrote the code, researched and drafted the articles, and gathered the church data. A person decides what gets made, reviews it, and chooses what goes live.

AI can make mistakes, so every answer links its sources for you to check. If something is wrong, please tell us and it will be fixed.

The tools are the Tree of Life, Your First Mass, the Church Finder, the Parish Website Studio and the Parish Scheduler. All of them are free, with no ads, nothing to pay for and no tracking.

Privacy

We collect as little as we can. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Some things are kept in your own browser (its “local storage”) so the tools remember your place; you can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Tree of Life

Your browser remembers which questions you have read, and the name and email you last typed, so you don't have to type them again. If you post a comment, its text and the name you give are public. If you send private feedback, only we can read it; an email address is optional and used only to reply to you. You can delete your own comments from the same browser.

Church Finder

Searching happens in your browser. When you type a town, the text is sent to a public place-name search (Photon, or Nominatim as a backup). If you let it use your location, that stays on your device. Details you add about a church are public; an email you leave with them is never shown and is used only if we need to ask you about it.

Parish Scheduler

Stores what a parish puts into it: the parish name, each volunteer's name, roles and schedule, and an email or mobile number if one is entered. Only people with a personal link to that parish can see it. A coordinator can remove a person, and can delete the whole parish with everything in it. Nothing is ever sent to volunteers automatically.

Parish Website Studio

Everything you type stays on your device and in the file you download. Nothing is sent to us.

Your First Mass

Your browser remembers which step you reached. Nothing else is kept.

Services the pages use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the sites. Like any web host it sees visitors' internet addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Supabase stores comments, feedback, church updates and the scheduler's data.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts; maps from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap; paintings, photos and church facts from Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata and Wikipedia; small code libraries from cdnjs and unpkg. Each of these sees your internet address when your browser loads from it.

Keeping and deleting

Comments stay until you or we remove them. Private feedback and church updates are kept only as long as they help improve the tools. To have anything about you removed, or to see what we hold, write to us. The tools are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Not an official source

These tools are not run by, or endorsed by, the Catholic Church, the Holy See, any bishops' conference, diocese or parish. The articles try to present Catholic teaching faithfully and link the Church's own documents; where they differ, the Church's documents are the authority.

Mass and confession times can change. Always check with the parish, especially on holy days and holidays.

Contact

The quickest way to reach us is the “Still have a question?” box under any question on the Tree of Life. It goes privately to us, not to the public comments. Leave an email if you would like a reply. For a church's details, use “Add a detail” or “Report a problem” on that church in the Church Finder.
